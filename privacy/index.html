Wake & Water Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Wake & Water ("the app") is an alarm clock and water tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your alarms, wake-up history, water log, custom drinks and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The Home Screen widget reads a summary of today's water from a shared container on the same iPhone. We do not run servers for the app and we never receive these records. Apart from the advertising and analytics described below, nothing about how you use the app leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in the app:

  • Saved to Health: the water and caffeine you log, so they appear in the Health app.
  • Read from Health: your sleep, only to show how long you slept. The app does not read any other health data.

Health data stays on your iPhone and in Apple Health. It is never sent to us, never used for advertising or analytics, and never shared with third parties. You can change access anytime in the Health app › Sharing › Apps › Wake & Water. Data the app saved to Health stays in Health if you delete the app; you can remove it in the Health app.

Permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): lets the app schedule alarms that ring while your iPhone is locked, silenced or in a Focus.
  • Notifications: water reminders and goal updates, scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Camera: used only during the Barcode Scan mission to read the code you chose, and during the Drink Water mission to check that you're holding a cup. Images are analyzed on device by Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion & Fitness: used only during the Shake and Steps missions to count shakes and steps. This data is not stored or shared.

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, the related feature is unavailable and the rest of the app keeps working.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob runs an auction in which these ad partners can also bid to show an ad: AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, InMobi and Moloco. Google and these partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to personalize ads. You can change this anytime in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. In both cases you can review your choice anytime in the app under Settings › Privacy › Privacy Choices, and the app passes your choice on to each ad partner above.

If you use "Report an ad", the app opens an email to us with the reason you chose, the ad's type, placement and time, any note you add, and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use the app and to improve it. It sends usage events such as:

  • App opens, screens viewed and onboarding steps.
  • Creating or editing an alarm: the alarm type, which missions it uses, how many days it repeats and whether snooze is on.
  • Finishing a wake-up: how many times you snoozed, how long it took to get up and how long the missions took.
  • Logging a drink: the drink type, the amount and where it was logged (app, widget or notification).
  • Paywall views, purchases, restores and subscription status.
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks and estimated ad revenue.

These events include a random app instance identifier created by Firebase. They don't include your name, email, alarm labels, custom phrases, the times your alarms are set for, or any data from Apple Health. Google processes this data under its privacy policy. Analytics are turned off for users under 13.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the bug. A report contains the app and iOS version, the device model, the code that was running and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. It doesn't contain your alarms, water log, Health data or anything you typed. Crash reports are used only to keep the app working and are not used for advertising.

Age range

Where Apple's Declared Age Range feature applies to you (for example in places whose laws require age checks), the app asks iOS for your age range. We receive only a range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18+), never your birthdate, and it stays on your iPhone. If you're under 18, ads are limited to content suitable for teens and are not personalized, and the app doesn't ask for tracking permission. If you're under 13, ads are child-directed and analytics are turned off.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.
  • Turn off Health access, notifications, camera or motion in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Change ad choices in the app under Settings › Privacy › Privacy Choices.

Your rights in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the GDPR (or the UK GDPR or Swiss data protection law) applies to the personal data described above.

  • Controller: the developer of Wake & Water, as named on the app's App Store page, reachable at phuongthaodev640@gmail.com. Google and the ad partners listed above are independent controllers for the data they collect.
  • Legal bases: your consent for personalized ads, tracking (ATT) and the ad partners' use of your data; our legitimate interests in understanding and improving the app (analytics), keeping it working (crash reports) and showing non-personalized ads that keep it free; performance of a contract for purchases, processed by Apple.
  • Retention: analytics events are kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months); crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Data stored only on your iPhone stays until you delete it or the app.
  • International transfers: Google and some ad partners process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Your rights: you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), and receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw consent anytime in the app under Settings › Privacy › Privacy Choices without affecting earlier processing. We don't run servers, and the data held by Google and the ad partners is linked only to random identifiers, so we may not be able to find data about you; deleting the app resets the Firebase identifiers, and resetting your Advertising Identifier (or denying tracking) stops it from being linked to new ad data.
  • Complaints: you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work.

To exercise these rights, email phuongthaodev640@gmail.com. We reply within one month.
